# Code of Conduct and Workplace Ethics (EMS-POL-004)

## 1. Professional Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of integrity, mutual respect, professional decorum, and collaboration. Discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, age, or disability is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Information Security and Confidentiality
1. **Confidential Information**:
   - Company source code, employee personal data, compensation numbers, financial projections, and customer lists are proprietary and strictly confidential.
   - Unauthorized disclosure of another employee's personal contact details or compensation package violates company policy and confidentiality agreements.
2. **Access Rights and RBAC**:
   - System users may only access organizational records within their defined Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) boundaries. Sharing administrative or privileged credentials is an automatic violation.

## 3. Disciplinary Procedures
- Violations of workplace ethics are handled through fair, progressive disciplinary procedures:
  1. Informal Counseling / Verbal Guidance
  2. Formal Written Warning
  3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) or Suspension
  4. Termination of Employment (subject to human HR committee review; never executed by automated algorithms).
