# Travel, Meals, and Expense Reimbursement Policy (EMS-POL-005)

## 1. Business Travel Eligibility
All business-related travel must be pre-approved by the employee's Department Head or authorized Finance Director prior to booking flights or accommodation.

## 2. Daily Per Diem and Allowances
- **Tier 1 Metro Cities**: Up to $100 / ₹4,000 per day for meals and incidental local transport.
- **Tier 2 Cities & Regional Centers**: Up to $75 / ₹2,500 per day for meals and incidental local transport.
- **Hotel Accommodation**: Standard room bookings within pre-approved corporate partner rate bands.

## 3. Expense Filing and Reimbursement Timelines
- Official expense claims along with legible tax invoices or digital receipts must be submitted via the EMS Portal within **30 calendar days** of completing travel.
- Approved claims are reimbursed in the subsequent bi-weekly or monthly payroll disbursement.
- Personal expenses, alcohol, and traffic fines are non-reimbursable.
