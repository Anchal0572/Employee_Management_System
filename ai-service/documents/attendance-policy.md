# Working Hours and Attendance Policy (EMS-POL-002)

## 1. Standard Working Hours
The standard working schedule across company facilities is Monday through Friday, from **09:00 AM to 06:00 PM** (40 hours per week), inclusive of a 60-minute lunch break and two optional 15-minute wellness breaks.

## 2. Punctuality and Grace Period
1. **Clock-In Grace Period**:
   - Employees are expected to record check-in at or before 09:00 AM.
   - A grace window of **15 minutes** (up to 09:15 AM) is permitted without penalty.
2. **Late Arrival Classification**:
   - Check-ins recorded between **09:16 AM and 11:00 AM** are designated as **Late Arrivals**.
   - Check-ins recorded after **11:00 AM** are automatically classified as a **Half-Day Present** unless pre-authorized by the department manager.
3. **Punctuality Review Threshold**:
   - Accumulating **3 or more late arrivals** within a single calendar month triggers an automated advisory notification to the employee and direct manager.

## 3. Remote and Flexible Attendance
- Eligible hybrid and remote employees must register their digital attendance via the EMS Self-Service portal upon commencing work duties.
- Employees must ensure proper logging of both check-in and check-out to calculate total active hours accurately.

## 4. Overtime Guidelines
- Overtime (OT) is applicable only for eligible roles and requires prior written or electronic approval from the department supervisor.
- A minimum duration of 2 consecutive working hours beyond 06:00 PM is required to qualify for compensatory time-off or overtime stipends.

## 5. Unexcused Absence
- Failure to log attendance or notify the supervisor for two consecutive business days without an approved leave request will be marked as Absenteeism and referred to HR for wellness verification.
