# Compensation and Payroll Policy (EMS-POL-003)

## 1. Payroll Cycle and Disbursement Schedule
- Salaries are calculated on a monthly cycle ending on the last calendar day of each month.
- Salary disbursement is executed on the **last working day of each calendar month** via direct bank transfer to the employee's registered account.
- In months where the last calendar day falls on a weekend or public holiday, disbursement occurs on the preceding business day.

## 2. Salary Structure and Deductions
Monthly employee compensation is composed of:
1. **Earnings**:
   - Basic Salary (typically 50% of Fixed CTC)
   - House Rent Allowance (HRA) (40-50% of Basic depending on metro categorization)
   - Special / Conveyance Allowances
   - Performance Bonus / Incentives (where contractually applicable)
2. **Standard Statutory Deductions**:
   - Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) based on declared income tax regime.
   - Employee Provident Fund (PF): 12% of Basic salary, matched with an employer contribution.
   - Professional Tax (as mandated by local state legislation).
   - Medical and Health Insurance employee premium share.

## 3. Payslip Access and Verification
- Digital payslips are generated and made available on the EMS portal immediately following payment processing.
- Employees can view, download, and print official encrypted PDF payslips directly from `/payroll`.
- Any payroll discrepancies or tax deduction queries must be reported to the Finance / Payroll desk within 7 calendar days of issuance.

## 4. Annual Increments and Appraisal
- Performance appraisals are conducted annually in Q4 (October - November).
- Salary adjustments and promotion increments take effect on January 1st of the following calendar year.
