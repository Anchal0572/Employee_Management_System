# Comprehensive Company Leave Policy (EMS-POL-001)

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees across all departments. The objective of this policy is to support workforce well-being, work-life balance, and provide transparent procedures for scheduling and taking time off.

## 2. Leave Entitlements
Employees are credited with the following annual leave allowances upon joining, prorated for the initial calendar year:
- **Earned Leave (Annual Vacation)**: 18 days per calendar year. Earned at the rate of 1.5 days per month of completed service.
- **Sick Leave**: 12 days per calendar year. Intended for personal illness, medical consultations, and recuperation.
- **Casual Leave**: 10 days per calendar year. For personal affairs, administrative errands, or unexpected personal commitments.
- **Emergency Leave**: 5 days per calendar year. Reserved for unforeseen medical or domestic emergencies.
- **Maternity Leave**: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for female employees, as mandated by statutory labor standards.
- **Paternity Leave**: 15 consecutive calendar days of paid leave within the first 6 months of childbirth or adoption.
- **Bereavement Leave**: Up to 5 consecutive working days in the event of the loss of an immediate family member.

## 3. Application and Approval Guidelines
1. **Advance Notice**:
   - For leaves of 1 to 2 days, submission must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
   - For planned leaves exceeding 2 consecutive working days (e.g. vacation), applications must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance via the EMS Leave Portal.
   - For emergency or unexpected sick leaves, notice must be reported to the line manager and logged on the portal by 10:00 AM on the day of absence.
2. **Medical Certification**:
   - For sick leaves extending beyond 3 consecutive working days, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed practitioner must be uploaded to the portal upon return.
3. **Approval Authority**:
   - Department Managers are designated reviewers for their direct reports. Requests exceeding 10 consecutive days also require secondary approval from Human Resources.

## 4. Carryover and Encashment Rules
- **Carryover Limit**: A maximum of 10 unutilized Earned Leave days can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.
- **Lapse**: Any unutilized Casual and Sick leave quotas will expire automatically on December 31st and do NOT carry forward.
- **Leave Encashment**: Up to 5 unutilized Earned Leave days can be encashed during the December payroll cycle, provided the employee maintains a minimum reserve of 5 days.

## 5. Unpaid Leave and Loss of Pay (LOP)
- Absence without approved leave or exceeding allotted quotas will be categorized as Loss of Pay (LOP).
- Deductions for LOP are calculated as `(Gross Monthly Salary / Working Days in Month) * LOP Days` and reflected on the upcoming month's payslip.
